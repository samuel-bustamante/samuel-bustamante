## About Me

<table>
<tr>
<td width="68%" valign="middle">

I have a degree in **Software Engineering** and an MSc in **Web Engineering**, both from the University of Oviedo. I'm based in **Spain** and work full-time as a researcher at **[WESO](https://www.weso.es/)** (Web Semantics Oviedo), where I'm about to start my **PhD**. My interest lies in **knowledge representation** and, in particular, in applying **knowledge graph** technologies to real-world problems.

Most of my work goes into **[rudof](https://github.com/rudof-project/rudof)**, an open-source Rust library that provides tools for working with **knowledge graphs**, which I help develop and maintain. **Rust** is my language of choice these days, but I'm happy to use whatever fits the problem best. I also really enjoy **architectural and design patterns** and the **infrastructure** side of software: building systems that are not just *correct*, but well-structured, easily maintainable and reliable.
